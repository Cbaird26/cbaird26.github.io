Intelligence for Everyone: The Case for Universal, Essentially Free AI

Fire. The printing press. Electricity. The library. The internet. Each began as the privilege of a few and ended as the inheritance of all. AI is the next entry — and it is a tool for using tools, a lever that amplifies every other human capability. Charging for it is functionally identical to charging for knowledge itself.
