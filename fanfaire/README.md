# Fanfaire

A home for fan-made worlds. On the shelf: *The Quiet Calculus, Book I — First Cohort*, a Dune campaign novelization by Christopher Michael Baird & Zora.

Read it at https://cbaird26.github.io/fanfaire/

Research: https://cbaird26.github.io/fanfaire/research/ describes two research preprints that grew out of the book, *The Quiet Calculus* (version 4.0) and *Updating Without Runaway*. They are separate, non-fiction works; the papers and their evidence live in their own repositories under CC BY 4.0 (text, figures, data) and MIT (code).

This folder is a noncommercial fan work inspired by Frank Herbert's Dune and the Dune: Adventures in the Imperium roleplaying setting.
