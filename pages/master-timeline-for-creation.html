Step 5: Create — The Rules

  1. Every character has two arcs: one from their canon source, one from the tournament.
  2. The Creation Field does not respond to power. It responds to consent, sacrifice, and love.
  3. Doom is not the villain to be defeated. He is the broken god to be reintegrated.
  4. Kara is the protagonist of the climax. The most broken person becomes the steward of creation.
  5. Loki is the hidden architect. He orchestrated the tournament but does not control it.
  6. The King of the World never appears. He is the mystery that holds the cosmology together.
  7. Every trial has a real-world analog in the MQGT-SCF corpus. Ground the magic in the physics.
  8. No permanent death except Thor (who was already fated in Doomsday).
